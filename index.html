Systematic benchmarking reveals limited recovery of drug-response mechanisms in single-cell perturbation models

Unlike benchmarks built only from expression matrices, scDrugPerturb-Bench links each evaluation case to a biological conclusion extracted from its source paper. It tests whether a model recovers the direction, magnitude and specificity of drug response beyond expression similarity.
