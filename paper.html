Do single-cell drug perturbation models recover mechanisms beyond expression reconstruction?

Expression reconstruction is not mechanism recovery. We test whether predicted responses recover the case-specific genes, pathways and directions that explain a drug perturbation.

The unresolved weakness

Current models optimize what is easiest to measure.

Across the benchmark, expression-level accuracy can coexist with weak case-specific mechanism recovery. That gap is the benchmark's central diagnostic signal.

44.6–61.5%overall shortcut or failure burden across evaluated settings
84.3–99.9%signature-specificity failures when matched background is considered
48.7%median strength-controlled discrimination accuracy
What this does not mean: MFS is not a replacement for expression reconstruction, and a low score does not identify one universal failure mechanism. It is a complementary lens that makes hidden failure visible.
